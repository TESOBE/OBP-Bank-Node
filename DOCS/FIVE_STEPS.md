# The Five Steps — plain-language captions

Text for a non-technical audience, written to accompany screenshots of the
UI (one caption per section of the overview page).

## Intro line

This screen follows a payment from one bank to another, top to bottom.

## Per-step captions

**1 · Send** — Someone asks their bank to pay a person at another bank, in
another country. The form is the payment order: who is paying, who should
receive the money, and how much.

**2 · Promise** — The sending bank takes the money from its customer's account
and publishes a promise on a public ledger that it now owes this payment to the
other bank. The promise is a fingerprint of the payment, not the payment itself
— no names or amounts are visible to outsiders.

**3 · Credit** — The receiving bank checks that the details it was sent match
the published promise, and pays its customer straight away. The person receives
their money in seconds, even though the banks have not yet moved any money
between themselves.

**4 · Position** — Each bank keeps a running total of what it has promised but
not yet paid to the other bank. This is the score between the two banks since
they last settled up.

**5 · Settle** — At any time, either bank can settle. Payments going in each
direction cancel each other out, and only the difference is transferred, in one
payment. Every promise covered by it is then marked as settled.

## Single-paragraph version

The page shows one payment's journey. A customer orders a payment (Send); their
bank takes the money and publicly promises to cover it (Promise); the receiving
bank checks the promise and pays the recipient immediately (Credit); each bank
keeps score of what it owes the other (Position); and whenever they choose, the
banks settle up — payments in both directions cancel out and only the
difference actually moves (Settle).
